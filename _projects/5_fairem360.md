Entity matching decides when two records refer to the same real-world entity, and its errors are not spread evenly across groups. **FairEM360** gives practitioners one place to audit matchers for unfair behaviour, explain where the disparities come from, and try mitigation strategies.

- Demo paper in the **Proceedings of the VLDB Endowment (VLDB 2024)**.
- [Video walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=euNuleF2P94).

Joint work with Nima Shahbazi, Dr. Abolfazl Asudeh, Dr. Fatemeh Nargesian and Dr. Divesh Srivastava.
