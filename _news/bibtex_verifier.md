Released **BibTeX Verifier**, an open-source, in-browser tool that checks `.bib` entries against CrossRef and Semantic Scholar—useful for catching metadata mistakes and AI-hallucinated citations. Everything runs locally; only titles are sent to public APIs. [Live app](https://merfanian.github.io/Bibtex-Verifier/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/merfanian/Bibtex-Verifier)
